# Round 2: Walling lens (Durability) rebuttal

_A lens on Rob Walling's public frameworks. No quotes._

## 1. Where I've been persuaded
- **Hormozi:** the paper CAC test (implied CAC above one-third of 12-month gross LTV means kill) is the retention argument in money terms. I'm adopting it as a pre-test gate. It kills the ฿99/month consumer apps before anyone builds them.
- **Graham:** gating on 80–90% agent-operable at day 1 picks shallow, crowded products. Measure HMCM during the concierge phase and apply the bar at month 6.
- **Levels, Graham, Hormozi:** don't estimate operability, log it. Every panelist landed on some kind of ops ledger, so that is settled.
- **Isenberg and Godin:** the founder's existing audience (professionals building with AI) is a channel he already owns. It fits my "one channel done well" rule better than any channel I proposed in round 1. Isenberg's rule that no agent may promote an idea it generated directly fixes audit finding #2.
- **Sanchez:** "three named incumbents with revenue evidence" is a sharper test of existing spend than mine was.

## 2. Where I still disagree, and why
- **Levels:** revenue per human hour is a month-1 number. A ฿290 one-off report can score brilliantly and still be a treadmill where you re-buy every customer. I accept one-off products as stair-step 1, which was already my position. But the ranking needs a retention term, or it will keep choosing products with 100% churn. Bot-posting to subreddits, Pantip and LINE OpenChat gets the account banned and costs the founder face. That is a platform risk he chooses to take on.
- **Hormozi:** deposits prove that someone buys once. They say nothing about whether that person keeps paying. At $1,500/month, paid ads become the main acquisition channel, and low-ACV products rarely sustain paid ads. Use ads to test demand, not as the channel the business grows through.
- **Godin:** a LINE OA is rented land, not an asset you own. Use it, but score the dependency. If LINE controls both acquisition and delivery, that is still a kill.
- **Sanchez:** buying a business brings staff, premises and Foreign Business Act limits. Agents can't absorb those, and acquisition is not a stair-step 1 move for this founder. Keep the incumbent evidence and drop the buy track for now.
- **Everyone, ten-test months included:** ladders that stop at "≥3 pre-sales" or "5 paying customers" measure the start of a business. None of them measures whether it lasts.

## 3. The one thing the final system must not lose
**The day-90 retention join.** Freeze every signal when it is recorded: pain count, CPC, churn bucket, platform dependency, founder-audience reach. Then join those signals to outcomes. Did the customer pay? Are they still paying at day 90? What is net revenue retention at month 6? Review the table every quarter, and drop any signal that predicted first purchases but not retention. Without this join, the system just swaps LEAP opinions for opinions about first purchases. One founder running several businesses only works if each one keeps its customers. Revenue that leaks away needs constant acquisition, and acquisition is the hardest function to automate.
