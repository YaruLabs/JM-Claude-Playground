# Round 2: Isenberg lens (rebuttal)

_A lens on Greg Isenberg's public frameworks, not the person. No quotes are his._

## 1. Where I've been persuaded
- **Levels:** "community costs human hours, price them." Fair. Founder posting time goes in the ops ledger.
- **Hormozi:** sign-ups were too soft as my L3. A refundable deposit is the real rung, and CPC is a cheap willingness-to-pay check before anything is built.
- **Godin:** Thais and expats are two audiences, so pick one per test. Also, the founder's own students (professionals building with AI) are the warmest crowd he has. Godin applied my lens better than I did.
- **Walling:** churn bucket and platform risk as tie-breakers.
- **Sanchez:** incumbent revenue is stronger paid-substitute evidence than gig listings alone. Google Maps 1-star clusters join the harvest.

## 2. Where I still disagree, and why
- **Levels, the posting bot.** A bot posting to subreddits, Pantip and LINE OpenChat gets banned and burns the founder's name. Agents draft; the human posts.
- **Hormozi, $1,500/mo of ads plus cold DMs.** Paid ads test whether the ad works, not whether a crowd exists. Free post first; ฿3–5k of ads only after it shows pull. Cold DMs at volume are spam (Godin is right).
- **Hormozi, cutting Apify first.** Thai pain lives in TikTok comments and Pantip. Cut Perplexity first.
- **Graham, cutting TikTok as a research source.** Comment sections are the richest free Thai complaint feed there is.
- **Walling, "community adds platform dependence."** Only if you rent it. A LINE OA list you own plus an email list is the hedge.
- **Sanchez, buying businesses.** Staff, premises and foreign-ownership limits make it a year-two move. HN as an anti-signal is half right: crowded launches mean skip, but "how do you handle X" threads are gold.

## 3. The one thing the final system must not lose
**A named crowd, reachable for free, before any build.** Every dossier must name 3 places where the first 100 buyers already gather (r/Bangkok, a specific expat FB group, a Pantip room, the founder's own students), plus the human who will post there. Without it you get a calibrated factory of products nobody sees.
